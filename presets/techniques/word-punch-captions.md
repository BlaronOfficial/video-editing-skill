# Word-punch captions

**Use for**: talking-head shorts where retention matters (Reels, TikTok, Shorts).

**Engine**: built in. Caption style `karaoke_pop` or `hormozi`, knobs and word keys.

**Recipe**
- 1-3 words per caption (`caption_words` 2-3), uppercase Latin, never uppercase Bangla.
- Emphasis: the 1-2 words per sentence that carry meaning (numbers, verbs of change, the payoff word):
  `caption_emphasis` op or `captions.emphasis_keys`. Not more than one in four words, or nothing stands out.
- One emoji every 6-10 s on an emotional beat (`caption_emoji`), never two in a row.
- Position above the platform UI: `caption_y` about -0.42 on 9:16 (check the safe zone in the Studio).
- Pair with a punch-in (zoom 1.08-1.15 over the line) on every second or third emphasis word, and a
  soft pop SFX only on the emoji beats.
